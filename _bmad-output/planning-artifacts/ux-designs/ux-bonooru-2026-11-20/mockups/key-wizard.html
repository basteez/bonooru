Wiring rules
  1. Connect only GP0, GP1 and GND. Never 5V, never 3V3.
  2. Rewire only with both USB cables unplugged.
  3. Once the boards work, leave the wiring as it is.
  4. On a breadboard, keep the 5V and 3V3 rows empty and don’t use the power rails. A wire one row off lands on 5V.
