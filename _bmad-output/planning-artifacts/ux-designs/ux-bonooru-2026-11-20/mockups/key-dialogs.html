Key screen · Dialogs and banners

Hybrid versions of the Play readiness confirm, the dialogue-style interruption banner, the end-of-song card, the required-update blocking dialog and the recommended-update banner. Song: “Ode to Joy.mid” (public domain).

Readiness confirm

Shown on Play from cursor / Play from start / Resume unless “Don’t ask again” is ticked. Enter = Play (focused), Esc = Cancel (and STOP, harmless when idle). One modal at a time over the scrim.

Interruption banner and end-of-song card

Both sit over the note view only. Left: playback stopped at 1:12 (USB suspend); cursor stays at the stop point; Resume goes through confirm + countdown. Right: PLAYBACK_DONE; the card auto-dismisses after 6 s (timer pauses on hover); no sound.

Required update (blocking)

Protocol mismatch detected via INFO. The LEDs show the boards’ current versions. “Update now” (focused, Enter) opens the wizard at the right flash step. “Not now” (Esc) closes the dialog: both Play buttons and the gamepad stay disabled until the boards are updated, while Listen, preview and editing keep working.
