Ocarina Bot — visual directions

Three complete looks for the main screen (layout Variant A, Ode to Joy.mid loaded), each evoking the mood of the Ocarina of Time remake menus without copying any of its assets. Every ornament, the controller silhouette and the button glyphs are original shapes. Windows are drawn at 1100 × 690 (≈ 1280 × 800 scaled).

Fingerings come from docs/ocarina-input-mechanics.md (community hypothesis, not yet verified in game). D = D-pad, S = left stick.

1

Lantern

Dark, warm wood panels carry the whole window, like the in-game equipment and options screens at night; parchment-gold appears only in the top strip, with a scalloped scrollwork edge. Gold labels and amber active segments guide the eye, and the selected thing always gets the same glowing white outline, so focus is unmistakable even when you glance over from the Switch.

Fonts Cinzel (titles) + Fira Sans (UI), both OFL 1.1 · Key hexes #2a1d12 panel · #e3a832 amber · #c9a86a parchment-gold · #ffffff glow

egui feasibility Rects, octagon polygons and strokes in Painter; the white glow is 2–3 stacked translucent strokes; the scalloped edge is a row of painted circles.

Parchment

The whole app is a sheet of parchment, like the controls sheet and the ocarina song card: dark ink text, a near-black controller silhouette with callout-style key tags, and umber rules with small flourishes. The Staff view is the hero — each note starts with its button glyph on ink lines — and selection is a red-ink accent rather than a glow, which keeps the page calm and bright in daylight.

Fonts Alegreya SC (titles) + Alegreya Sans (UI), both OFL 1.1 · Key hexes #efe2c0 parchment · #2a1f14 ink · #6b4a2a umber · #a8322a red ink

egui feasibility Flat fills and 1 px strokes only; flourishes and corner brackets are short cubic-Bézier paths (CubicBezierShape); a faint parchment texture can be a single bundled noise image or skipped.

Hybrid

A dark frame with the focused content on a parchment card: the piano roll sits on parchment inside corner brackets, while controls, status and messages stay dark. Messages use the translucent dialogue-box look with a small name tab, and selection glows amber. It has the strongest hierarchy of the three — your eye goes straight to the music.

Fonts Cormorant Garamond (titles) + Nunito Sans (UI), both OFL 1.1 · Key hexes #16110c frame · #ecdcb2 card · #f0b43c amber glow · #b8432f song warning

egui feasibility A Frame with a parchment fill for the centre panel and four painted bracket paths; translucent banners are a Color32 with alpha over the roll; the amber glow is stacked strokes.
