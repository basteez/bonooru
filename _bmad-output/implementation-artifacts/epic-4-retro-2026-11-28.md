## 2. What went well

The owner's view: "everything; the bench runs were an excellent test".

- **The benches found what host tests cannot:**
  - H.33: an occluded window draws no frames on macOS. This led to the bench checklist item "keep the window visible" and to moving the pad's safety logic into `App::logic` in 4.8.
  - H.36: the pause menu does not open in ocarina free play, and every reply typed in the terminal blurs Bonooru.
  - Both are now runbook gotchas.
- **The dry run and tool review before every bench (E2-A1, carried on) paid off again.** Reviews caught runbook errors before any owner time was spent:
  - 4.4: four pre-bench findings, including an unplug that passed without a physical unplug;
  - 4.5: H.33 step 5 timed at the wrong event;
  - 4.7: H.34 judged a log-line order the two threads do not guarantee;
  - 4.8: H.35 step 1 timed the owner's hand, and three steps had the wrong line order.
- **Every Epic 3 rule was applied:**
  - an AC → measurement table in every story with bench numbers (E3-A1);
  - an instrument review with a red proof per verdict (E3-A2);
  - a bench start checklist in every rig block (E3-A3).
- **Owner eye checks turned into concrete design changes:**
  - the label-to-detail gap became a 7 pt token (4.5);
  - hover and pressed states on every button (4.7);
  - 4.6, 4.8 and 4.9 needed no changes.
- **Safety numbers:**
  - STOP ACK 0.8–2.4 ms after the key;
  - every pad release path (Esc, click elsewhere, Cmd+Tab, Cmd+H, unplug) verified on the real Switch with OoT.

## 3. Challenges

- **Windows/Linux debt (the owner's main concern).** Every cross-OS bench row of the epic (H.28–H.36) is deferred to #43, and both M4 and M5 wait on it. The owner has no Windows or Linux machine, so this is a hardware constraint, not a slip. It still grows each epic, and M7 asks for T.1–T.5 on three OS.
- **Checks that cannot fail** were the most frequent review finding, in 8 of 10 stories. Examples:
  - 4.1: a smoke verdict always printed `ok`;
  - 4.4: smoke `worker=0`;
  - 4.5: `g16_dead_worker_unknown`;
  - 4.6: `g18_hint`;
  - 4.8: a queue-full test that exercised a copy of the send code, not the app.

  E3-A2 covered bench tools only.
- **CI flakes on the slow macOS and Windows runners**, six in the epic:
  - `g16_staleness_without_snapshot` (`1f24b3e`)
  - `g13_stalled_worker_is_visible` (`9a3b3cc`)
  - `g18_no_nagging` (`2ed1344`)
  - the G.23 rig, pre-emptively (`90ab7ef`)
  - `g25_h36_dry_run` (`dc280e9`)
  - `g3_every_focusable_node_shows_its_ring` (`f102f45`)

  The causes repeat: tight wall-clock bounds, time references taken after the action, waiting on state but not on the log line, and kittest's default of 4 steps.
- **Logic that ran only in `ui`:**
  - 4.8 (high): a held A survived Cmd+H;
  - 4.9: a key-up dropped in eframe's logic-only pass;
  - 4.6: the worker drain.
- **Keyboard interactions grew subtle:** Esc = STOP, Space consumed at frame start, Enter on STOP, the gamepad focus trap and its keys. Epic 5 adds Space for Listen, Cmd+O and text fields.
- **Issue #45:** a restarted `bonooru` hangs in `open()` while the previous process is still closing the port. It is worked around at the bench ("never restart while one is running"), not fixed.

## 5. Key insights

1. Safety logic must live in `App::logic`: eframe skips `ui` for hidden or occluded windows. This applies to Epic 6 playback, Resume and STOP.
2. Keyboard ownership needs one owner before Epic 5 adds Listen on Space, Cmd+O and text fields.
3. A red proof is the only reliable guard against checks that cannot fail. It should cover every new test, not only bench tools.
4. Slow CI runners fail in the same few ways. The rules that avoid them belong in every story.
5. The Windows/Linux gap can be partly closed without buying machines: an Apple Silicon Mac can run Linux ARM (and Windows 11 ARM) in a VM with USB passthrough.

## 6. Epic 5 preview: "Load a song and hear it on the PC" (M6)

Stories 5.1–5.13: profile module, text score parser, MIDI import, mono reduction and range fit, fingering and timing compile, official-song detection, open a song in the GUI, Roll view, fit proposal, transpose and tempo, Listen, song rail, Settings modal. Nothing is sent to the Switch.

Builds on Epic 4:
- the 4.3 painting helpers and `Modals` (5.8, 5.13);
- 4.2 `Role::Canvas` (5.8);
- the 4.7 bottom-bar slot, and Space consumed at frame start (5.11);
- the 4.9 `layout::is_compact` threshold (5.12);
- the 4.5 strip (5.13);
- the Esc = STOP contract.

Risks:
- **Space:** 4.7 consumes it; 5.11 needs it for Listen (see E4-A2).
- **Shortcuts:** Cmd/Ctrl+O and text fields meet the 4.9 gamepad focus rules.
- **New dependencies:** the `midly` licence, `libasound2-dev` for `rodio`/cpal in Linux CI, and `rfd`'s Linux backend (E4-A6).
- **Cross-OS checks:** audio and the native file dialog need them, which ties into E4-A1.

**Significant discoveries:** none change Epic 5's scope. The plan stands.
