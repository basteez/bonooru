# Story 4.8: Virtual gamepad with mouse

Status: in-progress

## Story

As the owner,
I want an on-screen Pro Controller I can click,
so that I can navigate the game to ocarina free play from the PC (FR-11, FR-22).

Origin: `epics.md` Epic 4, Story 4.8 (UX-DR35 pad drawing, UX-DR36 mouse semantics and PING, UX-DR38 gating hints, UX-DR64 caption, AD-5, AD-7, AD-8, AD-16; FR-11, FR-22; M5 exit "reach ocarina free play with the on-screen gamepad"), plus the bench line Story 4.7 added to its epic text ("an input held on the on-screen pad is released on the Switch by Esc and by STOP; `stop after … mode=idle` in the log"). It builds on Story 4.3 (painting helpers: convex fills, `paint::stroke`, `quad_points`, symbols ⌫ ↵, the basic AccessKit level), 4.4 (the serial worker, `WorkerCommand`, `LogHandle`, `SimRig`/`FakePort`/`ocb-sim`), 4.5 (the LEDs and their decision table, which the gate now reads; the rig block format, H.33), 4.6 (`gating`, `Block`, `widgets::inline_hint`, C.27 "nothing written while incompatible", and its deferred review findings 14–16, which land here), and 4.7 (STOP, Esc read at frame start, `App::request_stop`, `ButtonState` hover/pressed for every button, the H.34 bench and the forward notes for this story). It carries the Epic 3 retro rules E3-A1 (AC → measurement table below), E3-A2 (no bench tool is new or changed; the new `pad` and `mode` log lines are the bench instrument and each verdict they can show has a host test proving both values) and E3-A3 (rig block with the bench start checklist, dry run against `ocb-sim` before the bench).

**Scope: host code + docs + a macOS bench with OoT running (H.35, about 25 minutes) + an owner eye check. No firmware change, no wire change, no core (`ocb-core`) change.** In scope: the gamepad panel at full size (panel tab, caption, the original Pro-Controller-like silhouette with its 18 controls, key chips, legend, hint under the pad); mouse semantics (press = press, release = release, a click is a tap of at least 50 ms, sticks follow the drag and spring back); `WorkerCommand::SetState` and its handling in the worker (SET_STATE in DIRECT mode; PING when idle comes from the core session); the gate extended with the link reasons (worker, bridge, controller, Switch) and the deferred 4.6 findings 14–16; release rules (window blur, STOP/Esc, the gate turning blocked, the controller leaving DIRECT by itself); AccessKit nodes for every control; `pad` and `mode` log lines; host tests (C.29, G.17 update, G.22, G.23) and H.35. Out of scope (Story 4.9): panel focus, the inset amber ring and "Keys active" chip, the keyboard mapping, the focus-lock filter, the compact variant below 1280 px, the `Role::Group` panel node; (Epic 6): the lit-key visualizer and the "Locked · live view" chip during countdown and Play; (Epic 8): "Set up / Reflash…" next to the bridge hint; issue #45 itself (this story must not make it worse); Windows and Linux runs (issue #43).

## Acceptance Criteria

Numbers marked *(story)* are set by this story; every number is measured as listed in the AC → measurement table.

1. **The gamepad panel (UX-DR35, UX-DR64).** Inside the gamepad region (300 pt wide, Story 4.1; unchanged at every window width until Story 4.9 adds the compact variant), with 10 pt padding *(mock `.right{padding:10px}`)*, top to bottom: the panel tab "Gamepad" (`typography.panel-tab`, ink on parchment, top corners `rounded.sm`, over a 1 pt `rim` underline across the panel, mock `.ph`); the caption "Use it to reach ocarina free play." (`typography.small`, `text-muted`, 4 pt above and below, mock `.sub`); the pad (280 × 200 pt, horizontally centred, 6 pt above and below, mock `.padwrap`); the legend list (six rows, mock `.legend`); and, only while the gate blocks the gamepad, the inline hint 8 pt under the legend (mock `.ihint{margin-top:8px}`). At 1024 × 680 and 1280 × 800, ppp 1 and 2, everything stays inside the gamepad region and nothing overlaps.
2. **The silhouette and controls (UX-DR35, DESIGN.md › Virtual gamepad).** Geometry from `mockups/key-empty-state.html` (the 280 × 200 pad svg, coordinates in Dev Notes › Pad geometry), colours from the `DESIGN.md` tokens (the mock's hexes are not used): the body (`gamepad-body`: `rule` fill, 1.5 pt `rim` outline) is filled as a union of convex shapes and outlined by one closed stroked path following the mock's body outline; ZL, ZR, L, R as rounded rects above the body; two sticks (17 pt `well` ring with a 1 pt `rim` stroke + 11 pt knob); the D-pad (two rects forming a cross, arms 14 × 14 pt, `rounded.xs`, in a 22 pt `well` circle, the four arrows painted as triangles); A B X Y (10 pt circles in a 31 pt `well` circle, letters in `typography.key-chip`-sized bold text per Dev Notes); −, +, Capture (square with a dot), Home (a convex house shape in a circle); every control `gamepad-control` (`control` fill, 1 pt `rim` border, `text` foreground). Key chips (`gamepad-key-chip`: `well` fill, 1 pt `rim`, `text`, `typography.key-chip`, radius 3, 13 pt high) beside the keyboard-mapped controls: Z, Q, E, C, K, H, L, J, ⌫ (painted), ↵ (painted) and one "WASD · arrows" chip under the D-pad; "mouse" in `text-muted` next to each stick. Every filled polygon is convex; strokes are floored at 1 physical pixel (`paint::stroke`).
3. **The legend.** Six rows, 12.5 pt (`typography.small`), each with the control name left in `parchment` at weight 600 and the keys right in `text`, 3 pt × 4 pt row padding, a 1 pt `well` divider under each row *(mock `.legend`)*: "D-pad" / "W A S D or arrows"; "Y B X A" / "H J K L"; "L · R" / "Q · E"; "ZL · ZR" / "Z · C"; "+ · −" / "Enter · Backspace"; "Sticks, Home, Capture" / "mouse". The chips and legend describe the keys of Story 4.9; in this story keys do nothing on the pad (Open questions 14).
4. **Mouse: press, hold, tap (UX-DR36).** With the gate open, a primary-button press on a button control (A, B, X, Y, D-pad up/down/left/right, L, R, ZL, ZR, −, +, Home, Capture) presses it in the frame the press arrives; the release presses nothing new and releases it, but never earlier than **50 ms** after the press *(story; U7 min press 33.3 ms + one 60 Hz frame of margin, Story 1.4)*, so a click is a tap of at least 50 ms and a hold lasts as long as the button is held. A press that drags out of the control keeps it held until the release (egui `is_pointer_button_down_on`). Only one control is held by the mouse at a time. For circular controls the press counts only inside the circle (the A/B/X/Y bounding rects overlap at their corners). A press while the gate is blocked presses nothing (AC 9).
5. **Sticks follow the drag and spring back (UX-DR36).** A press on a stick's well starts a drag; each frame the stick value follows the pointer's offset from the well centre, full deflection at **17 pt** *(story; the well radius)*, clamped to a circle, no dead zone, axis mapping `axis(t) = 128 + round(127·t)` for `t ≥ 0` and `128 + round(128·t)` for `t < 0` (x right = larger `lx`/`rx`; y down = larger `ly`/`ry`, since `ly` 0x00 = up); the knob is drawn moved by the offset scaled to its 6 pt travel (17 − 11). On release the stick returns to 0x80/0x80 at once.
6. **Each change sends `SET_STATE` (AD-7, AD-16).** The pad keeps one canonical `State` (buttons, hat from the D-pad, four axes, `flags` 0). Whenever it changes, the app sends `WorkerCommand::SetState(state)` with `try_send` (never blocking); nothing is sent while it does not change (a held button or a still stick sends nothing; the core session sends PING every `app_timeout_ms / 4` while DIRECT is active, so the controller's app timeout never fires while the app runs). If `try_send` fails (`Full`, `Disconnected`, no worker), the current state is sent again on the next frame until it goes through (last state wins; a lost release can never leave an input held). The worker hands every `SetState` to `Session::set_state` in command order in the pass it is taken, so the SET_STATE frame goes out in that same pass; one `pad sent` line per frame handed over.
7. **The worker refuses what the controller would refuse, and writes nothing then (defence in depth; C.27).** `SetState` with no port, or a session in `Opening`, `Incompatible`, `Closed` or `Stopping`, or a last STATUS with `link_ok` 0 (or none yet), or `console_ready` 0, writes nothing and logs one `pad dropped reason=<…>` line (Dev Notes › Log lines). C.27 stays green unchanged.
8. **Release rules.** Every input the pad holds is released, and the pointer press that held it is spent (no new press until the button is released and pressed again), when: (a) the window loses focus (`Event::WindowFocused(false)` / `InputState::focused` false): one `SET_STATE` NEUTRAL; (b) STOP is requested by Esc or the STOP button: no `SET_STATE` (STOP itself releases everything, AD-8); a `SET_STATE` racing a pending STOP is dropped by the worker (`reason=stopping`), and the user presses again (4.7 forward note); (c) the gate turns blocked while the pad's last sent state is not NEUTRAL: STOP is requested (`App::request_stop(StopSource::Gamepad)`), because `SET_STATE` would get `NAK NOT_READY`/`NO_LINK` and leave the input applied; (d) the STATUS mode seen by the UI goes from DIRECT to IDLE without a request (the controller aborted: app timeout, USB event, link loss): nothing is sent (the controller is already neutral). One `pad release` line per released control or stick with its reason.
9. **Gating (UX-DR38; 4.6 forward notes).** The gamepad is enabled only while the worker runs, the three LEDs are `ok` and no firmware update is required. Otherwise the gate names one reason, first match wins: `worker` (LED reason `WorkerStalled`/`WorkerEnded`) › `no_bridge` (PC↔Bridge reason `NotFound`) › `bridge_not_ready` (PC↔Bridge not `ok` for any other reason) › `firmware_update` (`required_update`, as Story 4.6) › `controller_link` (Bridge↔Controller not `ok`) › `switch_not_ready` (Switch not `ok`). While blocked, a click on any control sends nothing and writes one `pad ignored control=<name> reason=<reason>` line per press, and the inline hint for the reason is shown under the legend (Copy table). Play gets the same reasons in this story (Epic 6 adds the song reasons). STOP stays ungated (never a `Block`).
10. **The `gate` line gives each its own reason (4.6 deferred finding 14).** `gate gamepad=<on|off> gamepad_reason=<r|none> play=<on|off> play_reason=<r|none> controller_proto=<n|-> app_proto=<n|->`, one line per change of the gate (as Story 4.6).
11. **The inline hint respects its rect (4.6 deferred finding 15).** `widgets::inline_hint` never paints or reports a node rect outside the rect it is given: a text taller than the rect is clipped to it. The hint moves under the pad (`main_screen::hint_rect` replaced by the pad layout's hint rect).
12. **Every gate and gamepad string is checked for online wording (4.6 deferred finding 16).** G.17's check iterates every `Block` variant through an exhaustive list (a new variant without an entry fails to compile), and every gamepad string (tab, caption, chips, legend, control names, hints) is checked against `leds::BANNED_WORDS`; the caption alone is exempt from the phrase "free play" (it is an instruction to the user, not a claim about the game state, which is what the LED rule bans; Open questions 11).
13. **Not in the Tab ring.** Every control is sensed with `Sense::CLICK | Sense::DRAG` (buttons) or `Sense::DRAG` (sticks), never `FOCUSABLE`: Tab order stays LED × 3 → STOP (Story 4.7 G.20 `g20_tab_order` unchanged). A press on the pad does not take keyboard focus (egui surrenders a focused widget's focus only on a click, i.e. at the release).
14. **AccessKit (Story 4.2: basic).** Each button control is one `Role::Button` node named "A", "B", "X", "Y", "D-pad up", "D-pad down", "D-pad left", "D-pad right", "L", "R", "ZL", "ZR", "Minus", "Plus", "Home", "Capture", without the `Focus` action; an AccessKit `Click` is a tap (press, then release 50 ms later), checked in kittest with `Node::click_accesskit()` (not `click()`). Each stick is one `Role::Slider` node named "Left stick" / "Right stick" (AccessKit 0.25 has no two-axis role), with no click or value requirement. The tab, caption and each legend row are `Role::Label` nodes; chips and symbols add no node (decorative; the legend says the same in words).
15. **Visual states.** With the gate open, a hovered control and a held control use the shared `ui::paint::ButtonState` tints of Story 4.7 (`components.state-layer`: hover 10 % toward `text`, pressed 20 % toward black, on fill and border); a held control stays tinted for the whole hold including the 50 ms tap; a dragged stick's knob is drawn pressed. While the gate is blocked no hover or pressed tint is shown (the hint says why).
16. **Input in the first pass only, one source of truth for STOP.** The pad reads pointer input in the frame's first pass only (Story 4.6/4.7: eframe gives input events to the first pass). If Esc or the STOP button requests STOP in a frame, a pad press in the same frame is ignored and spent.
17. **Log lines.** The app writes `pad press|release|stick|ignored|command` lines and the worker `pad sent|dropped` and `mode` lines in the exact formats of Dev Notes › Log lines, through the existing `LogHandle`s (non-blocking). The app writes its `pad press` (and `stick … start`) line **before** the `try_send`, so in the log it always precedes the worker's `pad sent` for it. No line while nothing happens.
18. **No regressions, CI green.** `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --locked --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --locked --workspace`, `cargo deny --locked check licenses bans`, the G.8 scripts, the smoke run (`worker=` ≥ 1) and "ocb-sim never ships" stay green on ubuntu, windows and macos. G.1, G.3, G.10–G.21, C.23–C.28 keep passing (changes only where this story adds tokens, gamepad nodes, the new `gate` format or `gate`/`mode` lines in a dry run, with the reason in the Debug Log). Every new check has a red proof in the Debug Log.
19. **Docs, bench and eye check.** `EXPERIENCE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `docs/test-plan.md` (C.29, G.17 update, G.22, G.23, H.35 and its rig block), `epics.md` (Story 4.8 bench line points at H.35), `app/README.md` updated as in Task 10. H.35 runs on macOS with the dry run green on the bench commit (owner present, OoT running); Windows/Linux go to issue #43. The M5 gamepad exit (free play reached with the on-screen pad only) is recorded; `roadmap.md` M5 stays TODO (Story 4.9 and the cross-OS runs). The owner eye check of the pad is recorded.

## Dev Notes

### Scope in one paragraph

The wire and the core already know DIRECT: `Session::set_state(state)` queues a SET_STATE that never waits behind queued events, refuses locally what the controller would refuse (not connected, `Stopping`, `link_ok` 0 or no STATUS, a piece started, flags set), sets `direct`, and while `direct` the session sends PING whenever no app frame went out for `app_timeout_ms / 4` (250 ms with the default 1000) (`app/core/src/link/mod.rs`: `set_state`, `handle_timeout`, `ping_due`; `docs/link-protocol.md` "Rules the app link applies"). The controller enters DIRECT on the first SET_STATE, applies each one unmasked (AD-9 masks only PLAYING), aborts to IDLE after 1000 ms without an app frame or on a DTR falling edge (AD-5), and STOP/HELLO always bring it back to IDLE with NEUTRAL (AD-8). This story draws the pad, turns mouse gestures into canonical states, sends them through a new worker command, extends the gate so clicks are ignored (with a hint) whenever the three lights are not on, releases everything on blur, STOP, a gate change or a controller abort, and checks it all on the real Switch with OoT running, including the "held input released by Esc and by STOP" check moved here from Story 4.7.

### Decisions taken at story creation (change only with a reason in the Debug Log; the owner can overrule any)

- **D1. One canonical `State` in the app, sent on change only.** The pad computes the full state (16 buttons → bits/hat, two sticks) and sends it whenever it differs from the last one sent; the worker never merges states. A failed `try_send` keeps it pending and resends the current state next frame, so the last state always wins and a lost release cannot leave an input held. No coalescing in the worker: every `SetState` goes to the session in order (a stick drag sends at most one per UI frame; at ~120 Hz × 11 bytes the UART at 460800 baud has ample room).
- **D2. Tap = at least 50 ms.** A click shorter than a frame would otherwise press and release before the console's next 8 ms poll, or be shorter than the game's 2-frame minimum (Story 1.4 U7: 1 frame sounds 16–18/20, 2 frames 20/20). 50 ms = 3 frames at 60 Hz (U7 + one frame of margin, Story 1.5's convention). The press goes out at once; only the release waits. AccessKit click = the same tap.
- **D3. Press on pointer down, not on click.** Menus react to the press on a real pad; waiting for the click (release) would add the hold time to every input and make "hold" impossible to tell from "click".
- **D4. The gate reads the LEDs.** `gating::evaluate(snap, &leds)`: the LEDs already judge STATUS staleness (150 ms), the INFO grace, the worker's health and the NAKs; the gate takes their states, so "the gamepad works when the three lights are on" and never disagrees with the strip. `required_update` still comes from the snapshot (Story 4.6 rule).
- **D5. Release on gate block = STOP.** When the gate turns blocked while something is held, SET_STATE NEUTRAL may be refused (`NAK NOT_READY` / `NO_LINK` / local `NoLink`), leaving the input applied on the controller (an IN-poll stall does not abort DIRECT; `docs/link-protocol.md` › `console_ready`). STOP is never refused (AD-8) and is harmless without a session (Story 4.7). Logged as `stop requested source=gamepad`.
- **D6. Release on window blur = SET_STATE NEUTRAL.** The gate is open then, NEUTRAL is accepted, DIRECT stays (PING continues) and the next click needs no new mode change; this matches the pointer-up path.
- **D7. Drop the held set when the controller leaves DIRECT by itself.** Seen as a STATUS mode change DIRECT → IDLE in the UI's snapshot (app timeout, USB event, link loss): the Switch no longer sees the input, so the pad stops showing it as held and does not resend it; the user presses again. Only a DIRECT → IDLE transition counts (the first press is sent while STATUS still says IDLE).
- **D8. Worker drops what the controller would refuse.** Besides the session's own refusals, the worker drops `SetState` while `console_ready` is 0 (the controller would answer `NAK NOT_READY`; `docs/link-protocol.md` bans probing readiness with SET_STATE) and logs every drop; it never writes in `Incompatible` (C.27).
- **D9. Pressed look = state-layer tint, not "lit".** `gamepad-control-lit` (amber + glow) is the Play visualizer's (Epic 6); a held control uses Story 4.7's `ButtonState::Pressed` tint, hover the `Hovered` tint, both only while the gate is open.
- **D10. Blocked pad keeps its look, nodes stay enabled.** The mock's "Switch not ready" state draws the pad normally with the hint under it; clicks are ignored and logged (`pad ignored`), no tint reacts. AccessKit nodes stay enabled (the hint label says why). See Open questions 6.
- **D11. Full pad at every width until Story 4.9.** The gamepad region is 300 pt at every width (Story 4.1 `layout::regions`); the compact variant below 1280 is Story 4.9's. At 1024 × 680 the full panel fits (about 455 pt of the 548 pt region height).
- **D12. The panel tab "Gamepad" is drawn now.** It is the mock's header and the place where Story 4.9 puts "Keys active". (The Song panel's tab comes with Epic 5.)
- **D13. The caption may say "free play".** `leds::BANNED_WORDS` bans "free play" because an LED cannot know the game state (Story 4.5); the caption is an instruction from the epic AC (UX-DR64) and claims nothing. The exemption is only for the caption and only for that phrase; every other banned word still applies to it.
- **D14. No stick click (L3/R3).** Not in the epic AC's control list nor in the keyboard mapping; the game does not use them for navigation (U11). Open questions 4.
- **D15. A short bench is not enough here: H.35 is about 25 minutes with OoT running.** See Bench decision.

### Log lines (AC 17; kind tags `pad` and `mode`; the bench reads exactly these)

App (UI thread, `App::log`; `<c>` = `Control::log_name()`, `<s>` = `left|right`):
- `press control=<c> source=<mouse|accesskit>` — written before the `try_send` of the state it causes.
- `release control=<c> reason=<pointer_up|tap|window_blur|stop|gate|controller_idle>` — `tap`: the release waited for the 50 ms minimum (the pointer came up earlier, or an AccessKit tap).
- `stick=<s> start` — before the first `try_send` of the drag.
- `stick=<s> end reason=<pointer_up|window_blur|stop|gate|controller_idle>`.
- `ignored control=<c|stick_left|stick_right> reason=<worker|no_bridge|bridge_not_ready|firmware_update|controller_link|switch_not_ready|busy>` — one per press while the gate is blocked, or (`busy`, review 2026-11-27) while the pad already holds an input (a second press within a click's 50 ms tail, an AccessKit tap during a hold).
- `command=<queue_full|no_worker|worker_gone>` — only when a `try_send` fails (the state is resent next frame); at most one line per failure streak (written on the first failure, and again only after a success).

Worker (`WorkerCore::log`):
- `sent port=<p> buttons=0x<hhhh> hat=<n> lx=<n> ly=<n> rx=<n> ry=<n>` — `Session::set_state` returned `Ok`; the frame goes out in this pass, unless a `Stop` taken in the same pass follows it (the STOP clears unsent frames, AD-8: only STOP goes out).
- `dropped reason=<no_port|opening|incompatible|closed|stopping|no_link|not_ready|busy|bad_state|not_connected> port=<p|->[ action=stop]` — nothing written for the state; ` action=stop` (review 2026-11-27): it was a release (NEUTRAL) refused while the session is `Connected`, so the worker sent STOP instead (STOP releases everything and is accepted whatever the console does).

Worker, kind `mode`:
- `port=<p> from=<idle|direct|playing|0x<hh>> to=<…> abort_count=<n>` — a STATUS whose mode differs from the previous STATUS of the same session (none for the first STATUS after an open or a reboot).

Story 4.6/4.7 lines changed: `gate gamepad=<on|off> gamepad_reason=<r|none> play=<on|off> play_reason=<r|none> controller_proto=<n|-> app_proto=<n|->`; `stop requested source=<esc|button|gamepad> …`.

Patterns (`tests/common::line_matches` style; no regex crate): `press` + `control` OneOf[the 16 log names] + `source` OneOf[mouse, accesskit]; `release` + `control` + `reason` OneOf[…]; `stick=` OneOf[left, right] + `start` | `end` + `reason`; `ignored` + `control` + `reason` OneOf[the six block names]; `command` OneOf[…]; `sent` + `port` NonEmpty + `buttons` `0x` + 4 hex + `hat` Digits (0–8) + four axes Digits (0–255); `dropped` + `reason` OneOf[…] + `port`; `mode` + `port` + `from`/`to` OneOf[idle, direct, playing] or `0x<hh>` + `abort_count` Digits.
